read_available(max_bytes, timeout_sec=5)

Wait for one byte, then drain queued bytes without waiting for a block.

Streaming parsers retain and split the block. Quantize the waiting timeout to Windows’ millisecond resolution so tiny deadline changes don’t trigger SetCommState/SetCommTimeouts for every fragment of every packet.
